### The Revenue Leak

NordinTech, a family venture business, where they started in Manufacturing Industry to Filteration. They scaled to become a leading provider of water filtration systems and services.
It is a B2B business where it is unseen from online platforms like Google.
They were invisible where it mattered most: **organic search for high-intent keywords**.
Despite strong technical expertise, they weren't being found by decision-makers actively searching for solutions.
Their sales team were spending long cycles to close deals. Online visibility was their biggest challenge.

The compounding cost of invisibility:

- **Zero organic traffic:** Relying entirely on referrals and cold outreach—unsustainable for growth

- **Low-quality leads:** Without authority signals, inquiries focused on price instead of value

- **Long sales cycles:** Every prospect required extensive education before trusting the firm

- **Competitor advantage:** Competitors with authority sites captured market share by default

##### Estimated Cost of the Leak:

**Zero rankings** = missing 100% of organic search traffic in a high-value B2B niche

**Poor trust signals** = inquiries requiring 2-3x more sales effort

**Extended sales cycles** = fewer deals closed per quarter, higher customer acquisition costs
