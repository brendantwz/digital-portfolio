# Privacy Policy

What personal data I collect, why, and what you can ask me to do with it.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

## 1. Who is responsible

Brendan T., an independent technical consultant based in Malaysia, is responsible for personal data collected through brendantwz.com. Contact: [brendan@nixlab.my](mailto:brendan@nixlab.my).

## 2. What I collect

- **Enquiries:** the name, email, business name, platform, challenge description and referral source you submit through the contact form, or anything you send me by email or LinkedIn.

- **Technical data:** standard server logs kept by my hosting provider (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested), used for security and reliability.

I do not run advertising trackers on this site and I do not sell personal data.

## 3. Why I use it

- To reply to your enquiry and schedule a systems audit call.

- To prepare a proposal and, if we work together, to deliver and invoice the engagement.

- To keep the website secure and working.

## 4. Who processes it

Contact form submissions are delivered by a form-handling service, the site is hosted by a web hosting provider, and email is handled by my email provider. These providers process data only to deliver their service. I do not share your data with anyone else unless required by law.

## 5. How long I keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for as long as needed for the engagement and for legal, tax and accounting obligations.

## 6. Your rights

Under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 you can ask to access or correct your personal data, or ask me to stop processing it. Email [brendan@nixlab.my](mailto:brendan@nixlab.my) and I will respond within 21 days.

## 7. Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too. See also the [Terms of Service](https://brendantwz.com/terms.md).
